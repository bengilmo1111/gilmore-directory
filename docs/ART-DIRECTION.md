# Gilmore Games Directory — Art Direction

## Creative idea

**A family game clubhouse at the edge of a playful New Zealand landscape.**

The directory should feel handmade, energetic and welcoming: a place built by a family for their own games, rather than a polished corporate game portal.

## Personality

- Fun, curious and slightly scruffy.
- Bold enough for children, but not babyish.
- Warm, local and family-made.
- Playful motion and irregular shapes rather than slick glass panels.

## New Zealand references

Use a light touch. The setting should quietly feel like Aotearoa New Zealand without becoming a tourism poster or borrowing culturally significant Māori motifs as decoration.

Good references:

- rolling green hills
- distant mountain silhouettes
- a winding blue river
- sheep in the landscape
- southern-night stars
- native-green foliage and loose fern-like flourishes
- plain language such as “Made in Aotearoa New Zealand”

Avoid:

- flags everywhere
- rugby clichés
- heavy use of koru, tā moko or other culturally significant forms
- faux-Māori display typography
- literal national branding

## Visual system

### Colour

- sky blue: `#6ec5e9`
- deep blue ink: `#15314b`
- hill green: `#67b85a`
- deep hill green: `#246b45`
- warm cream: `#fff8e7`
- paper white: `#fffdf6`
- sunshine yellow: `#ffd166`
- playful red: `#e84a5f`
- accent purple: `#8e5ccb`
- accent orange: `#f28c45`

Each game card can use one strong accent colour, but text and outlines should remain consistent.

### Typography

Use Fredoka for display headings and Nunito for body text. Headings should be chunky, rounded and compact. Body copy should remain highly readable.

### Shapes

- thick dark outlines
- soft but irregular corner radii
- slightly rotated cards and labels
- solid paper-like surfaces
- strong offset shadows
- no glassmorphism

### Illustration

Keep illustrations lightweight and achievable in HTML/CSS. Emoji can be used as friendly icons. Landscape elements should sit behind the content and never reduce readability.

## Page structure

1. Illustrated hero landscape.
2. Large hand-painted-style Gilmore Games sign.
3. Family player badges, including Alice.
4. A clear “Choose your next adventure” heading.
5. Tactile game cards with obvious play affordances.
6. A warm family-made footer.

## Interaction

- Cards lift and straighten on hover.
- Icons can wobble or grow gently.
- Clouds and sheep may move very slowly.
- Keyboard focus must be obvious.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility

- Maintain strong colour contrast.
- Keep all game cards keyboard accessible.
- Include a skip link.
- Decorative landscape elements must be hidden from assistive technology.
- Do not communicate game categories through colour alone.

## Art-direction test

A successful page should feel like this sentence:

> The Gilmores built a cheerful game shed somewhere between a Wellington back garden, a green paddock and a fantastical adventure map.
